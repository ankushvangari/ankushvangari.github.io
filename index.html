bob

bob is a private, personal AI assistant built and used by one person, its owner. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public. No sign-in is needed to read this page.

What bob does

  • Answers the owner's questions about travel plans, bookings and schedules over a private chat.
  • Reads the owner's own Gmail messages (read-only) to find things like flight and hotel confirmations.
  • Reads the owner's own Google Calendar (read-only) to plan around existing events.

Google data access

  • Gmail: read-only access (gmail.readonly). bob cannot send, delete, or modify email.
  • Google Calendar: read-only use. bob does not create, edit, or delete events.
  • Only the owner's Google account is connected. No other users' data is accessed.
