Privacy policy for bob

Last updated: October 8, 2026

bob ("the app") is a private, single-user personal assistant. Its only user is its owner, who also built and operates it. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how access can be removed.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): email messages and metadata (sender, subject, date, body) in the owner's own mailbox.
  • Google Calendar (read-only use): event titles, times and locations in the owner's own calendars.
  • Chat messages the owner sends to the app.

The app does not access data belonging to any other Google user.

2. How the data is used

  • Only to answer the owner's own requests: for example, finding a flight confirmation, listing upcoming trips, or checking for schedule conflicts.
  • The app never sends, modifies or deletes email, and never creates, changes or deletes calendar events.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

3. Storage and security

  • The app runs on the owner's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are held in an encrypted local credential vault and are never shared with the AI model or stored in chat history.
  • Email and calendar content is fetched on demand and is not copied into a separate database. The owner may keep short notes (for example, trip dates) in the app's local notes.
  • To answer a request, relevant text may be sent to the owner's AI model provider (Anthropic), under that provider's terms. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party, except as described in section 3 to answer the owner's own request.

5. Retention and deletion

  • The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further reads.
  • Local notes can be deleted by the owner at any time.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Contact: github.com/ankushvangari.
